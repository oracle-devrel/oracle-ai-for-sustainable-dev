# AR Privacy and Venue Checklist

- [ ] Obtain venue approval and review wearable-camera and attraction rules.
- [ ] Explain camera sensing even when media recording is off.
- [ ] Keep the device privacy indicator and in-Lens status visible.
- [ ] Default media recording and precise location sharing to off.
- [ ] Require explicit, revocable consent for each sensitive capability.
- [ ] Avoid face recognition and biometric identity matching.
- [ ] Process locally where possible; send only necessary data.
- [ ] Use TLS, production authentication, least privilege, and scoped tokens.
- [ ] Apply exact guest/agent scope before retrieval.
- [ ] Store a purpose, source, consent state, TTL, and audit event.
- [ ] Mark inferred, stale, or low-confidence information.
- [ ] Confirm reservations, purchases, or other consequential actions.
- [ ] Provide browser, audio, high-contrast, reduced-motion, and non-AR paths.
- [ ] Validate outdoor visibility, comfort, battery, heat, and distraction.
- [ ] Test permission prompts, ASR, camera frames, tracking, and revocation on hardware.
